ScreenshotMemory

Support

ScreenshotMemory is an Android-first app that discovers useful screenshots, understands them locally, and helps bring them back when they matter.

Getting started

ScreenshotMemory needs access to the relevant media on your device to discover screenshots. Android may ask you to allow that access when it is needed.

A screenshot is not appearing

Check that the screenshot is still stored on your device and that ScreenshotMemory has the media access it needs in Android settings. The exact availability of media can vary by Android version and permission choice.

Not seeing resurfacing notifications

Check Android’s notification settings for ScreenshotMemory. If notifications are disabled at the device or app level, Android will not show them.

Privacy

ScreenshotMemory is designed to process screenshots and OCR text on your device, without cloud upload, advertising, or tracking. Read the full Privacy Policy.
