ScreenshotMemory

Privacy Policy

ScreenshotMemory is designed to help you remember useful screenshots while keeping their processing on your device.

Summary

ScreenshotMemory is an Android-first mobile app that discovers screenshots on your device, processes their contents locally, and may resurface them when they could be useful. It does not require an account, cloud synchronization, advertising, or analytics/tracking SDKs.

Screenshots and OCR text are not uploaded to external services as part of ScreenshotMemory’s core operation.

Screenshot and media access

ScreenshotMemory needs access to relevant media on your device in order to discover screenshots. The app uses that access to identify screenshots available on the device; it is not intended to upload those screenshots to a cloud service.

Whether the app can access a particular screenshot also depends on the Android version, the permission you grant, and whether the file remains on your device.

On-device processing and local storage

ScreenshotMemory uses on-device OCR to understand text in screenshots. This processing is performed locally. Information the app needs to operate—such as its local understanding of screenshots and app settings—is stored locally on your device.

ScreenshotMemory does not upload screenshots or OCR text to external services, and it does not provide cloud synchronization.

Notifications

ScreenshotMemory may use Android notifications to resurface a screenshot. Android controls whether notifications are allowed. You can change notification permission and notification settings through your device’s Android settings.

No account, ads, analytics, or sale of information

  • No account is required to use ScreenshotMemory.
  • ScreenshotMemory does not use advertising.
  • ScreenshotMemory does not use analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • ScreenshotMemory does not sell personal information.

Your control and deletion

You can manage ScreenshotMemory’s access and notifications in Android settings. You can remove screenshots from your device using your device’s usual media controls. Removing the app removes its locally stored app data through Android’s normal uninstall behavior; Android may provide additional options to clear app storage before uninstalling.

Because ScreenshotMemory does not use an account or cloud synchronization, there is no separate cloud account or cloud copy for you to delete.
